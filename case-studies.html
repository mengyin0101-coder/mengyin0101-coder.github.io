01 / Year-round Sports PR

01 / 全年体育公关

HSBC China Junior Golf Program

汇丰青少年高尔夫发展计划

Year-round event communications highlighting junior golf development and the value of the competition programme.

通过全年赛事传播，持续呈现青少年高尔夫的发展与赛事价值。

Overview

项目概览

A junior golf development programme linking grassroots participation, graded competitions and international opportunities.

汇丰青少年于 2007 年启动，是国内唯一官方认证的完整青少年高尔夫人才培养体系，覆盖高尔夫普及、基层参与、分级赛事、全国积分排名与国际赛事机会。

My Role

我的角色

I led annual PR planning, media relations, social content and on-site media-centre operations.

统筹全年公关规划、媒体关系与社媒内容，并负责现场媒体中心管理，串联赛季传播与赛事现场执行。

Challenge

项目挑战

Sustain interest throughout the season and make the player development pathway easy to understand.

挑战在于如何贯穿整个赛季保持关注度，并清晰讲述从校园与训练营启蒙，到全国顶级赛事、再到国际赛事外卡机会的完整成长路径。

Strategy

传播策略

Connect player growth stories, trusted media and community participation to show HSBC’s long-term role in junior golf.

不把项目当作单一赛事系列传播，而是将其定位为长期的青少年高尔夫成长通道：串联官方赛事体系、球员成长故事、权威媒体背书、社群互动与赞助商价值。

Execution

项目执行

  • Annual PR review and next-year planning
  • Season launch communication planning
  • On-site media-centre operations
  • Media and creator relations
  • Social content and campaigns
  • 年度 PR 复盘与次年规划
  • 赛季启动传播策划
  • 现场媒体中心管理与媒体服务
  • 媒体、KOL 与合作伙伴关系维护
  • 社交媒体运营与 campaign 策划

Results / Evidence

成果与依据

Media monitoring recorded RMB 78.3M+ in media value, 244 invited media outlets and 1,993 articles in 2023. Jan–Sep 2024: RMB 95M+, 206 outlets and 1,538 articles.

2023 年全年媒体价值超过 7,830 万元，邀请发稿媒体 244 家、发稿总量 1,993 篇；2024 年 1 至 9 月媒体价值超过 9,500 万元，邀请发稿媒体 206 家、发稿总量 1,538 篇。

Key Learnings

关键收获

Player progress gives a season-long programme a stronger story than event announcements alone.

长期人才培养项目不能只发布赛事信息。传播体系需要让成长路径清晰可见，持续呈现球员进阶，也让合作伙伴在整个赛季持续感受到参与价值。

02 / Nationwide Brand Campaign & Community Activation

02 / 全国品牌 Campaign 与社群激活

Saucony Triumph 24

From campaign strategy and historic city routes to execution and performance review.

将新品上市转化为城市跑者可参与的品牌体验。

Overview

项目概览

Eight Saucony Run Club sessions across six Chinese cities, engaging urban runners and existing brand communities.

面向城市跑者、跑步社群与既有 Saucony Run Club 用户的全国活动。2026 年在北京、上海、深圳、南京、杭州与西安共落地 8 场。

My Role

我的角色

I led the project from strategy to recap: campaign concept, city-specific historic routes, experience design, execution planning and performance analysis.

主导活动全案，将产品舒适感转化为晨跑创意与城市参与机制，完成执行方案及活动复盘。

Challenge

项目挑战

Turn product comfort into a repeatable community experience that encourages participation, sharing and return visits.

挑战在于把产品的舒适感，转化为跑者愿意报名、再次参与并主动分享的理由，同时建立可在不同城市社群中稳定复制的活动机制。

Strategy

活动策略

Connect Saucony’s 1898 heritage with each city’s history through morning runs, landmark stories and shared photography.

以统一的“清晨仪式”为核心，再通过城市路线与社群氛围完成在地表达，让产品信息自然连接参与、内容传播与新会员转化。

Key Message

核心信息

Awake Ahead. Win in Comfort. links a morning ritual with product comfort and confidence in your own pace.

Awake Ahead. Win in Comfort. 将“先醒一步”、清晨自律与产品舒适感连接起来，把“胜利”诠释为从容掌控自身节奏的状态。

Execution

项目执行

  • “Awake Ahead” morning-run activation with city-landmark checkpoints
  • Polaroid and film-camera capture of participants' awake moments
  • Runner stories, Xiaohongshu (RED) content and UGC planning
  • SRC treasure-hunt and orienteering extension planning
  • “先醒一步”晨跑与城市地标打卡机制
  • 通过拍立得 / 胶片相机记录“先醒瞬间”
  • 跑者故事、小红书内容与 UGC 规划
  • SRC 寻宝 / 定向赛延展策划

Results / Evidence

成果与依据

582 participants, 192 new members and 33.0% member conversion. On Xiaohongshu (RED), 36 posts generated 2,394 interactions.

2026 年全国活动累计获得 5,486 次页面浏览、582 名参与者，带来 192 名新增会员，拉新转化率达 33.0%。社媒发布 36 篇小红书内容，获得 2,394 次互动，其中 7 篇互动量超过 100。

Key Learnings

关键复盘

Turn product benefits into a repeatable running experience, then measure participation and member conversion.

跑步品牌活动的价值，在于把产品利益转化为生活方式主张、可复制的城市路线机制与可衡量的会员转化。

Core Idea

核心创意

Comfort as a Morning Advantage

把舒适，变成清晨抢先一步的优势

01

Awake Ahead

先醒一步

Connect product comfort with the confidence and routine of an early morning run.

项目不只讲产品舒适，而是把舒适转化为一种状态：更早出发、更轻松行动、更从容地领先一天。

02

One Ritual, Six Cities

一个仪式，六城共鸣

Each city followed a different historic route, connecting local landmarks and stories with the same morning ritual.

晨跑、地标打卡和拍立得“先醒瞬间”构成统一仪式，再通过每座城市的路线、社群与内容表达完成本地化。

03 / Three-Destination Strategy & Event Proposals

03 / 概念案例与战略提案

National Geographic Trail Race国家地理 · 越野赛事策划

Three destination proposals for Guizhou, Chongqing and Zhoushan, integrating event concepts, tourism experiences, partnerships and delivery planning.

将目的地资源转化为连接越野赛事、户外节庆与品牌合作的项目提案。

Project Context

项目背景

One event platform, three distinct concepts: Hidden Guizhou, Into Chongqing and A Living Archipelago for Zhoushan.

分别为贵州、重庆与舟山开发方案，将国家地理的探索叙事与各地地形、文化和户外参与体验相结合。

Strategic Challenge

战略挑战

Balance destination value, sponsor benefits and a feasible participant experience.

提案需要同时面向政府端目的地价值和品牌赞助商商业价值，平衡文化叙事、赛事可行性和可衡量影响。

Concept Direction

概念方向

A “Festival of Wonder” combines trail racing, local culture and outdoor learning to encourage destination exploration.

项目将越野赛事延展为“惊奇生活节”：让运动成为探索自然、理解目的地，并形成重复到访理由的体验平台。

Programme Framework

项目框架

  • Destination storytelling and public-sector value
  • Trail race model and race categories
  • Outdoor festival and signature experiences
  • Operations, promotion and measurement planning
  • 目的地叙事与政府端价值
  • 越野赛事模型与组别
  • 户外生活节与标志性体验
  • 运营、传播与测量规划

Destination Value

目的地叙事逻辑

Connect local landscapes and culture with racing, storytelling and travel to build a recurring event.

将各地自然与文化资源连接到赛事参与、内容传播和目的地旅行，形成可持续的户外活动平台。

My Contribution

我的贡献

I developed all three proposals: positioning, event concepts, commercial estimates, communications and delivery planning.

搭建目的地策略、赛事与生活节框架及商业测算，将在地资源转化为参与体验与赞助商价值。

Key Learnings

关键收获

Connect the destination story to a feasible event and clear sponsor benefits.

一个目的地赛事提案，只有把文化意义、参与者体验、运营可行性和品牌合作价值设计为同一套系统，才真正具备说服力。

Concept Framework

概念框架

Three Destinations. One Platform.

三地概念，一个赛事平台

01

Hidden Guizhou

秘境贵州

Karst landscapes, heritage routes and local culture turn a race into deeper destination exploration.

以喀斯特地貌、遗产线路与在地文化，将赛事延展为深度探索。

02

Into Chongqing

纵深重庆

Connect urban steps and riverside paths with mountain and gorge experiences.

连接城市阶梯、滨江步道与山岭峡谷，从城市肌理跑进山水纵深。

03

A Living Archipelago

一部活的群岛志

Explore Zhoushan through island trails, maritime culture and ocean education.

以海岛越野、海洋文化与自然教育，走进真实的舟山群岛。

04 / Community Trail Series Review

04 / 社群越野跑系列复盘

HOKA Trail Running Series

A review of three trail events, assessing community participation and content performance.

通过三站赛事复盘，呈现社群参与及内容传播的表现。

Overview

项目概览

A review of HOKA's Run Wilder events in Yushan, Moganshan and Wangweishan, analysing event experiences, community participation and brand content performance.

复盘 HOKA「一起野外」虞山、莫干山和王位山三站活动，分析赛事体验、社群参与与品牌内容表现。

My Role

我的角色

I independently produced the full recap: three-event data analysis, content evaluation, insights, recommendations and report design.

独立完成整套复盘，涵盖三站数据分析、内容表现评估、洞察建议与报告呈现。

Challenge

项目挑战

Understand how trail conditions, creator profiles and runner feedback shaped the community and future event decisions.

复盘需识别三种不同赛道环境、达人类型和跑者反馈如何共同影响社群质量，并为后续活动决策提供依据。

Review Approach

复盘策略

I compared participation, completion, content, creators and runner feedback across three events to inform future planning.

从报名、参赛、完赛、内容表现、达人价值和跑者反馈六个维度对比三站，提出内容与社群运营建议。

Execution

项目执行

  • Compared Yushan, Moganshan and Wangweishan participation data
  • Reviewed WeChat and Xiaohongshu (RED) content performance
  • Mapped creator value across high-reach, high-engagement and everyday runner creators
  • Synthesised runner feedback, real trail conditions and event highlights
  • 对比虞山、莫干山和王位山三站参与数据
  • 复盘微信与小红书内容表现
  • 分析高覆盖、高互动与 KOC 达人价值
  • 整合跑者反馈、真实赛道体验和各站亮点

Results / Evidence

结果 / 证据

The series recorded 2,927 original registrations, 1,904 participants and 1,720 finishers—a 90.3% completion rate among participants. Social output totalled 228 posts and 17,381 interactions. The source recap reports an estimated combined reach of 1.555M across different channel measures.

三站系列累计报名 2,927 人、参赛 1,904 人、完赛 1,720 人，完赛率达 90.3%。社交内容共发布 228 篇，累计互动 17,381 次。源报告估算综合覆盖 155.5 万，包含不同渠道口径。

Key Learnings

关键收获

Recommendations covered routes, local culture, community experiences, content distribution and data review.

结合赛事数据与跑者体验，梳理路线体验、在地文化、社交场景、内容传播和数据复盘五个运营模块，为后续活动提供参考。
